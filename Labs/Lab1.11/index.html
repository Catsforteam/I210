Location

My hometown Avon is a town located in eastern Hendricks County, Indiana, just west of Indianapolis. It is situated five miles west of downtown Indianapolis and is considered a suburb of the city.

About

Avon's Community

Avon is a fast-growing suburban community west of Indianapolis, Indiana, known for its strong schools, vibrant parks, and family-friendly atmosphere, with a focus on natural preservation, as seen by its status as the first Tree City USA in Hendricks County.

Avon's Landmarks

Landmarks in the vicinity of Avon, Indiana include local spots like Avon Gardens, the Old Avon Village, and the Pine Grove Schoolhouse. For a broader perspective, consider attractions in nearby Indianapolis such as Lucas Oil Stadium, the Children's Museum of Indianapolis, and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.
